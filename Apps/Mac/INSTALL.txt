Cascade Console for Mac — install & first launch
==================================================

This build is UNIVERSAL: it runs natively on Intel Macs and on
Apple-silicon Macs. It needs macOS 14 or newer.

1. Drag CascadeConsole.app into your Applications folder
   (or just run it from this folder).

2. First launch: the app is ad-hoc signed (built on GitHub's free
   runners, not notarized by Apple), so Gatekeeper asks once:
   - Right-click (or Control-click) the app and choose "Open",
     then click "Open" in the dialog.
   - On macOS 15 and later, if the dialog has no "Open" button:
     open System Settings -> Privacy & Security, scroll down to
     the Security section, and click "Open Anyway" for
     Cascade Console. Then launch it again.
   You only have to do this once.

3. Your files: the app keeps its scripts, exports (.step/.stl)
   and projects (.cascadeproj) in its Documents folder — open the
   Files panel inside the app. macOS may ask permission for the
   app to access Documents; allow it so saves and exports work.

What you get: the full app — the native Open CASCADE engine (not
a demo/stub), the Metal 3D viewer, the command panel with
sliders, the parametric history, the part tree with folders and
name directories, and the text console for scripts, exports and
simulation. Same app as the iPad build, same sources.
